# Outside-the-Box Avenue Triage

**Status:** Research triage.  These routes were tested after the residual
atlas programme was demoted.  Each was stopped at its first structural or
finite falsifier rather than enlarged into another census.

## 1. Base conversion and binary digit complexity

The input

\[
a_n=(3^n-1)/2=[1^n]_3
\]

is unusually simple in base three.  The Collatz CQCA converts this input to
binary and constrains part of its short-term spacetime diagram.  This does not
control the accelerated payout word: ordinary binary digits and Collatz
parity digits are related by a mixing 2-adic conjugacy, not by digit sum.

For the hard exponent \(n=471\), the base-conversion front is exhausted at
cumulative halving coordinate 746, around accelerated odd step 385.  Descent
below \(2^{471}-1\) occurs only at odd step 732.  The unresolved part lies in
the CQCA's lower rectangle, where the forward Collatz complexity remains.

**Decision:** closed unless a repunit-specific theorem converts unary ternary
boundary data into a payout-surplus bound beyond the conversion front.

## 2. Primitive divisors of \(3^n-1\)

For a prime \(p\mid a_n\), the affine identity reduces to

\[
2^{E_K}x_K\equiv c_K\pmod p.
\]

The endpoint is free, and primitive divisors can be much smaller than the
pre-descent state scale.  Combining all factors merely reconstructs the
known affine identity.

**Decision:** closed; no new constraint survives reduction modulo the
repunit factors.

## 3. CQCA conservation and unary light cones

The local full-adder rule has the conservation equation

\[
s_0+c_0+s_1=s_2+2c_1.
\]

Summing it along a row gives the exact carry identity

\[
C(x)=2s_2(x)+1-s_2(f(x)).
\]

Scalar flux sums collapse to the existing affine bookkeeping.  Repeated
division of the homogeneous ternary bulk develops periods governed by powers
of two modulo powers of three, returning to the existing carry problem.

**Decision:** closed as a scalar or fixed-wallpaper argument.

## 4. Temporal merger grammars

Paired valuation histories of the 2333 exact mergers through \(n\le5001\)
were tested for a small suffix grammar.  At suffix length eight, 1987 of 2013
histories were distinct.  Cumulative valuation-difference paths have few sign
changes, but primitive descending pairs have the same or stronger property.

**Decision:** a local rewrite dictionary or sign-excursion stack is not the
missing temporal compression.

## 5. Descent-transfer fan

The exact inequality transfer and its bounded-fan falsifier are recorded in
`repunit_descent_transfer_fan.md`.  Gap two succeeds often, but gaps through
80 add no genuine certificate on its residual and leave \(n=471\) untouched.

**Decision:** closed unless failure itself is proved to force merger or
surplus.

## 6. Additive energy of payout shells

Low additive energy creates many distinct shell differences, but those
integers occupy an exponentially large ambient interval and need not meet a
reachable correction set \(\mathcal A(i,u)\).  High energy gives structured
sets; the balanced blocked family is already such a structured obstruction.
Neither alternative gives a deterministic descent or merger.

**Decision:** do not run an energy census without a proved incidence or
capacity implication.

## 7. The induced record map

During a valuation-one record step,

\[
R_{K+1}=3R_K,
\qquad B_{K+1}=B_K,
\qquad D_{K+1}=D_K+\log_2(3/2).
\]

All six blocked-diffuse records on \(n=471\) occur on such steps.  Sampling
only records therefore retains the existing payout ledger plus a trivial
linear drift.  Sampling payout epochs returns the original valuation word.

**Decision:** closed unless the state contains a new arithmetic coordinate,
in which case closure must be proved adversarially.

## 8. Zero-tail local recovery

At the last diffuse record of \(n=471\), the next five valuations are

\[
(2,4,1,1,6),
\]

which immediately repair the 4.23-bit record deficit.  A proposed general
rule conditioned on primitivity, record extremality, and
\(E_K>\operatorname{bitlen}(n)\) was tested through \(n\le5001\).

It has no finite failure before eventual descent, but its natural
\(O(D_K)\) scale collapses: at \(n=2189,K=12\), the record depth is only
\(D_K\approx2.0196\), while recovery to nonpositive deficit takes 53 odd
steps.  Finite-cylinder plateau constructions give no reason for a universal
constant.

**Decision:** closed as a local recovery theorem.

## 9. Recent parity-vector results

The 2026 sharp finitary parity-density and paradoxical-sequence enumeration
theorems are counting results.  Their continued-fraction classification is a
numerical conjecture, and their stopping target is the initial value rather
than the much lower Mersenne threshold.  PCD9 already shows why finite-word
rarity cannot exclude repunit cylinders.

**Decision:** no current theorem transfers to the residual.

## 10. Surviving requirement

Every failed route omits at least one of the following:

1. the least positive exponent representative of the valuation cylinder;
2. exact nonmembership in the reachable correction layers;
3. primitivity or prior-merger exclusion;
4. strict record extremality.

The next acceptable avenue must couple these data in one deterministic
statement.  A useful target would have the form:

> **Least-representative transversality target.**  At a primitive strict
> record, a correction target that misses every admissible reachable layer
> cannot simultaneously have a small positive exponent representative and
> remain inside the pre-descent Archimedean window.

This is an arithmetic correlation theorem, not a finite-language cover.  No
larger experiment is warranted until an exact inequality, incidence bound,
or unlikely-intersection formulation makes one of its conclusions a descent
or a smaller-exponent merge.

## 11. Transversality theorem audit

The target does not fit the standard arithmetic transversality theorems in
its present form.

- A (2)-adic logarithmic-form estimate applies effectively to
  (v_2(3^m+d)) when (d) is fixed or has controlled logarithmic height.
  For a generic record prefix the reduced enemy (d=d_K) has
  (log|d_K|\asymp E_K).  The resulting variable-height bound feeds the
  unknown valuation scale back into its own upper bound and is weaker than
  the required estimate.
- The quantitative (S)-unit theorem bounds nondegenerate solutions of a
  fixed linear equation with a fixed number of variables in a finite-rank
  multiplicative group.  A correction has one summand for every shortcut
  step, so the dimension grows with the proposed counterexample.  Moreover
  the free endpoint is not an (S)-unit.
- Dynamical Mordell--Lang concerns the visits of one fixed algebraic orbit to
  one fixed subvariety.  Here both the affine branch and the correction
  target change with the valuation word; the shortcut map is also
  piecewise, with an unbounded valuation choice.  Encoding all branches
  merely reconstructs the original Collatz system.

These are not technical omissions that a larger census can repair.  Each is
a failed hypothesis at the same scale as the desired conclusion.

There is also an exact finite-memory barrier for modular correction
obstructions.  For a source word of length (i\ge s),

\[
A_i=-3^i+2\sum_{t=0}^{i-1}3^{i-1-t}2^{E_t}
\]

reduced modulo (3^s) loses every term with (t<i-s).  The surviving last
(s) terms depend on the earlier prefix only through

\[
E_{i-s}\pmod{\operatorname{ord}_{3^s}(2)}
=E_{i-s}\pmod{2\cdot3^{s-1}}.
\]

Consequently, for each fixed (s), membership or nonmembership modulo
(3^s) is a bounded-state predicate: the state is one cumulative-valuation
residue followed by at most (s) valuation parts.  The same localization
applies to a shell target because it is an affine combination of a high-word
correction and a power of two.

> **Bounded-depth closure.** A uniformly bounded obstruction depth cannot be
> the missing least-representative correlation.  It is another finite-word
> language, while every compatible finite valuation word already has an odd
> repunit exponent cylinder.

The (n=471) prototype, whose first failing digit is (3^{13}), therefore
does not indicate a new global invariant.  A viable correction route would
first have to prove that obstruction depth is unbounded along every putative
primitive record branch, and then couple that growing depth to the exponent
representative.  Neither standard (p)-adic logarithmic forms, fixed-rank
(S)-unit equations, nor dynamical Mordell--Lang supplies that coupling.

**Decision:** close least-representative/correction transversality as a main
avenue.  Reopen only after an independent theorem forces unbounded
obstruction depth; do not enlarge the modular fan census.

## 12. Two-prime rigidity and the actual-source anti-diagonal

A tempting refinement is to combine a depth-(r) congruence modulo (3^r)
with the source itinerary congruence modulo (2^{u+1}).  If the same
correction difference satisfied both and were smaller than
(2^{u+1}3^r), the Chinese remainder theorem would force equality and hence
a merger.

The (n=471) prototype exposes the false premise.  Its formal source-layer
approximant at length (i=30) has total valuation (u=40) and survives
modulo (3^{12}).  The actual aligned smaller exponent is

\[
m=(n+j+1)-i=469.
\]

Its first 30 shortcut valuations total (59), not (40), and its correction
difference from the shell target is not even divisible by (3).  The
(3)-adic and (2)-adic approximations belong to different source words, so
their moduli cannot be multiplied.

One can instead scan the actual aligned sources on the fixed diagonal,
(m=d-i).  For the admissible lengths (i=30,32,\ldots,40) of the prototype,

\[
E_i(d-i)-u=(19,19,46,46,46,46).
\]

This has no intermediate-value behaviour.  More importantly, the shift
(i\mapsto i+2), (m\mapsto m-2) is exactly the gap-two, time-shifted
same-diagonal comparison already studied by the synchronization-tree and
descent-transfer programmes.  The plateaus above are smaller-tail
synchronizations, not a new invariant.

**Decision:** close the two-prime product argument because its congruences
refer to different words.  Close the actual-source repair because it is the
existing gap-merger route in diagonal coordinates.

## 13. Hecke--Mahler strengthening and factor recurrence

The balanced critical itinerary already has a complete qualitative
discharge.  IEF10 excludes the exact mechanical word by periodic rational
approximants, and IEF11--IEF21 extend the mechanism to Sturmian intercepts
and broad low-discrepancy recurrent languages.  Proving the stronger
irrationality or transcendence of the critical (2)-adic Hecke--Mahler value
would not reduce the currently stated survivor profile.

The complementary word class has Diophantine exponent one, strong return
scarcity, and directionally visible critical-drift excursions.  It is
tempting to connect repeated factors to smaller-tail mergers.  This does not
produce a new exact implication.  Two occurrences of the same valuation
factor have the same affine multiplier, but their starting states remain
free; primitivity says precisely that the relevant states are unequal.
Without a correction equality, repetition gives only the eventually
periodic rational approximant already used by IEF11.

Likewise, superlinear factor complexity or entropy can make the correction
suffixes numerous without forcing any one of them to equal a reachable
aligned correction.  This is the same incidence gap that invalidated the
additive-energy route.

**Decision:** do not pursue stronger Hecke--Mahler results for the repunit
descent goal, and do not promote word complexity or return scarcity without
an exact descent-or-merger consequence.  At present no such consequence is
available.

## 14. Current stopping point

After the residual atlas, all tested replacements fall into one of four
already-exposed barriers:

1. finite-state information, which cannot control the least exponent
   representative;
2. density, entropy, or expansion information, which cannot eliminate an
   individual exceptional cylinder;
3. variable-height or variable-dimension Diophantine equations, for which
   standard effective theorems are tautological at the required scale; or
4. virtual or approximate collisions whose congruence data belong to a
   different word from the actual aligned smaller repunit source.

The only acceptable restart condition is now an exact lemma coupling a
putative survivor to an *actual* smaller repunit trajectory, with either
state equality or a monotone descent inequality.  Neither another residue
sieve nor another symbolic-language classification meets that condition.

## 15. Restart: the full actual-source transfer fan

The restart condition is met by the unrestricted descent-transfer fan. The
earlier gap-80 test was too narrow: testing every smaller odd repunit exponent
through $n\le5001$ raises exact transfer coverage to $96.6360\%$ and finds
nine genuine certificates whose smallest successful gap exceeds 80.

More importantly, the full fan exposes the same-diagonal payout-surplus
difference

\[
H_g=(E_s(n-g)-s)-(E_{s-g}(n)-(s-g)).
\]

Strict sign agrees with the exact transfer inequality on every one of more
than 1.8 million tested comparators; only two equality cases require the
affine correction. The sign law follows from the single storage-dominance
condition $0<R_i(n)<3^{n+i}$ at the compared states, verified through first
descent on every odd $n\le5001$.

**Decision:** reopen only this narrow avenue. The next proof target is the
first-descent storage-dominance lemma in
`repunit_descent_transfer_fan.md`. It couples actual smaller trajectories to
a monotone descent inequality and is not invalidated by the finite-language,
virtual-source, or variable-height barriers above.
